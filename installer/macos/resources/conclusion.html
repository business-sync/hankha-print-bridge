The Print Bridge is running

  1. Open the POS terminal on this Mac.
  2. Go to Settings → Printing.
  3. The Print Bridge card should read Print Bridge is running. Then press Search to find your printers.

To check it by hand: curl http://127.0.0.1:9200/health

To remove it later: sudo /usr/local/hankha/print-bridge/uninstall.sh
