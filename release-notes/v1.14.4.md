# 1.14.4 — kitchen tickets reach a printer that dozed off

**Drop-in upgrade** (`relay.json` / `printers.json` untouched).

## What was wrong

- **Kitchen and pizza tickets from the POS failed after a quiet spell.** A venue's log showed it
  all evening: `accept-…-HOT` and `accept-…-PIZZA` failed with `connect-timeout (none)`, and the
  very next ticket to the same printer 20–40 s later printed in 1 ms. Only the kitchen and pizza
  printers did it; the bill printer, used every few minutes, never did. Those printers go to
  sleep (or drop off Wi-Fi) after a few idle minutes and take several seconds to answer again.
- **The POS route gave up too early.** `POST /print` tried a 2 s connect, waited, tried another
  2 s connect, and stopped at about 4.8 s of the POS's 8 s. Relay jobs to the same printers
  connected on their retry after exactly 3020 ms, which is Windows' first SYN retransmit. A 2 s
  attempt is cut off before that retransmit is ever sent.
- **The websocket never connected on an installed bridge.** Every reconnect logged
  `relay.ws.handshake_failed status=101`: Bun's `node:http` reports the server's `101 Switching
  Protocols` as an ordinary response, so a successful upgrade read as a refusal. The relay fell
  back to long-polling, which works, so nothing visibly broke.

## What changed

- **Keep-warm.** Every minute the bridge connects to each enabled network printer and closes the
  connection again. It writes no bytes, so nothing prints. This keeps the printer, and this PC's
  ARP entry for it, awake. A printer whose lane is busy is skipped. `PRINT_BRIDGE_KEEPWARM_MS`
  sets the interval (default `60000`, `0` turns it off, minimum `5000`).
- **New log lines from keep-warm:** `printer.unreachable` when a printer stops answering,
  `printer.reachable` when it comes back (with how long it was gone), and `printer.slow` when it
  took over a second to answer, meaning it was asleep.
- **Staggered connects on `POST /print`.** The bridge opens a fresh connection every second and
  leaves the earlier ones open for their own retransmits. The ticket goes to the first one
  accepted and the others are closed before any byte is written. This continues until 1 s before
  the POS stops waiting (7 s of its 8 s). A ticket still can't print twice, and a failure is still
  `printed_certainty: none`. `queue.done` now records `connect_attempts` and `connected_after_ms`
  when more than one connection was needed.
- **Websocket on Bun.** The compiled bridge now uses Bun's own WebSocket client. A refused
  handshake is re-asked once over HTTP to read its status, so a revoked token (401) and a server
  without a socket route (404/426) are handled as before. Under Node (`npm run dev`, tests) the
  hand-written client is unchanged.

## What to look for after installing

- `app.config … keepwarm_ms=60000`.
- `relay: connected over websocket {event=relay.ws.open …}`, and `relay_transport=websocket` in
  `diag.snapshot`, instead of repeated `handshake_failed status=101`.
- No `print.sync.result status=502` for kitchen or pizza tickets. Any `printer.unreachable` names
  the printer and address that dropped off the network.
