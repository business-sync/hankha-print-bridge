# 1.14.8 — a kitchen printer's test slip looks like a kitchen ticket

**Drop-in upgrade** (`relay.json` / `printers.json` untouched). Only the test slip, its log line,
`/status` and one detail in the status page changed. Printing, the queue, keep-warm, `POST /print`,
`POST /jobs` and both relay transports behave exactly as in 1.14.7.

## What was wrong

- **Every test print was a customer receipt.** `POST /printers/:id/test` (the status page's Test
  button, and the POS printer console's "Print test slip") rendered the same sample on every
  receipt-type printer: `2x Test item 50,000`, `TOTAL LAK 50,000`, a barcode and a QR. At a venue
  on 2026-11-01, staff pressed Test on the pizza printer twice at 11:19 and read the result as
  the kitchen printer printing bills. The bridge log showed two 980-byte jobs and nothing else —
  980 bytes is exactly that sample for a printer named `pizza` — and no order printed anywhere
  near that time, so nothing had been misrouted. The registry's `role` tag was only ever reported
  upstream; the test route never looked at it.
- **The log could not say what a test print was.** Its result line was debug-only, so at the
  default level a test page was a bare `queue.submit` / `queue.done` pair.

## What changed

- **A kitchen test ticket.** `sampleKitchenTicket` (`src/samples.ts`): the same printer-facts
  block and ruler as before, then a station banner, `#TEST-0001` with the time, `2x Test item`
  with a modifier line, `1x Test item`, and "Kitchen ticket - no prices." No price, total,
  barcode or QR.
- **The slip follows what the printer is for.** `resolveSample` picks it, first match wins:
  an explicit `doc`; a `station` alone means kitchen; the registry `role` (`kitchen`/`bar` →
  kitchen ticket, `bar` with a `BAR` banner; `receipt` → customer sample); and for an untagged
  printer, the customer sample only on the **main receipt printer** (`default_receipt_printer` —
  "Use for bills" in the POS console — or the only receipt printer) and a kitchen ticket on every
  other printer. Most venues never tag roles, so without that last rule the bridge's own Test
  button on an untagged pizza printer still printed a bill-shaped slip. With several receipt
  printers and none named, untagged stays the customer sample. Label printers always get the label.
  - Trade-off: a second untagged bills printer (a two-till venue) now tests as a kitchen ticket.
    Tag it `receipt` (the POS console's "Sends" field) to get the customer sample on it.
- **`POST /printers/:id/test` takes an optional body** `{ doc?: "receipt" | "kitchen", station? }`.
  With no body the rule above decides. An unknown `doc`, or a `station` that is not
  a string of at most 24 characters, is `400 invalid-body` and nothing reaches the printer. The
  answer adds `sample: { doc, station }`. A POS that knows the printer is bound to a kitchen
  station sends `{ doc: "kitchen", station }` — needed because most venues never tag roles.
- **`print.test.result` is logged at info** on success, with `doc` and `station`:
  `print: test kitchen slip printed on pizza (PIZZA)`.
- **`/status` reports `role`** for each printer (null when untagged), and the status page shows
  it after `transport · type · language`.
- **`--test-print <id>` takes `--doc receipt|kitchen` and `--station <code>`**, validated by the
  same rules as the route.

The customer sample is byte-for-byte unchanged (979 / 980 / 982 bytes on an 80 mm ESC/POS
printer named `bill` / `pizza` / `kitchen`), so old log lines still read the same way.

## Verify after installing

1. On the bridge PC open <http://localhost:9200>. Press Test on a kitchen printer (tagged
   `kitchen`, or untagged and not the bills printer): a kitchen ticket comes out, with no total.
   Press Test on the bills printer: the customer sample, as before. A tagged printer shows its role
   in the row, e.g. `· kitchen`.
2. The log has `print: test kitchen slip printed on <id>` for it.
3. From the POS, Settings → Printing → bridge console → "Print test slip" on a printer bound to a
   kitchen station prints the kitchen ticket with that station's banner once the POS is updated;
   an older POS sends no body and gets the slip the rule above implies.
